Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Web mai Chart X ("Web mai Chart X", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Web mai Chart X web application and its Google Drive integration.

1. What Web mai Chart X Does

Web mai Chart X is a browser-based music game chart editor. The application can optionally integrate with Google Drive so that users can create, store, download, and open Web mai Chart X project files from their Google Drive.

2. Information We Access

Google Account Information

When you sign in with Google, Google may provide basic account information required to authenticate you, such as your Google account identifier and basic profile information.

Web mai Chart X does not require access to your Gmail, contacts, Google Photos, or other Google services unrelated to its functionality.

Google Drive Data

If you enable Google Drive integration, Web mai Chart X requests the following Google OAuth permissions:

  • drive.file — allows the application to access specific Google Drive files used with Web mai Chart X.
  • drive.install — allows Web mai Chart X to integrate with Google Drive's application and "Open with" functionality.

The application uses Google Drive access only to provide the cloud project functionality described in this policy.

3. How We Use Information

Information and Google Drive data are used to:

  • Authenticate you with Google.
  • Create and manage Web mai Chart X project files in Google Drive.
  • Download project files selected by you.
  • Open Web mai Chart X project files from Google Drive.
  • Provide synchronization and cloud storage features explicitly requested by you.
  • Maintain and improve the application's functionality and security.

We do not use Google Drive data for advertising, targeted advertising, profiling, credit assessment, or unrelated services.

4. How Google Drive Data Is Handled

Web mai Chart X is primarily a client-side web application. Google Drive files are transferred between your browser and Google's services using Google's APIs.

Web mai Chart X does not operate a server-side database that permanently stores copies of your Google Drive project files.

Project files downloaded from Google Drive are processed by your browser so that they can be opened by the editor.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or provide your Google Drive data to advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties.

Google Drive data is transmitted to Google services as necessary to provide the Google Drive integration requested by you.

We may disclose information when required by applicable law, legal process, or to protect the security and integrity of the service.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

Limited Use Disclosure

Web mai Chart X's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Data Retention

Web mai Chart X does not intentionally retain permanent server-side copies of your Google Drive project files.

Project files stored in Google Drive remain subject to your Google Drive account and Google's own data retention policies.

Browser-local data, such as cached application data or locally stored projects, may remain on your device until you remove it through your browser or the application's available controls.

8. Revoking Access

You can revoke Web mai Chart X's access to your Google account through your Google Account security settings. After access is revoked, Google Drive features requiring authorization will no longer work until you authorize the application again.

9. Security

Web mai Chart X uses HTTPS when communicating with the application and Google services. OAuth access tokens are handled by the browser and are not intentionally stored in a Web mai Chart X server-side database.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Users should also protect access to their Google account and devices.

10. Children's Privacy

Web mai Chart X is not specifically directed at children under the age required by applicable privacy laws. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information for purposes unrelated to providing the application.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its data practices, or applicable requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page indicates when the policy was most recently revised.
